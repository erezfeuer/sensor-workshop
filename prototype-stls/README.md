# Personal prototype print sets

These are separate from the standard workshop [production-stls](../production-stls/).

**[bare-scd30/](bare-scd30/)** is Erez's personal set for the **bare Sensirion SCD30** and Adafruit **TSL2591 QT** light board. Print **one of each of its three separate STLs**, at 100% scale in millimetres, keeping their supplied orientations. The shell and reinforced Feather frame match workshop v0.6; the sensor carrier is different.

[Assembly and extra hardware](../enclosure/variants/bare-scd30/README.md) · [Personal interactive model](../enclosure/variants/bare-scd30/model.html) · [Mounting drawing](../enclosure/variants/bare-scd30/mounting.pdf)

The carrier needs a seven-pin 2.54 mm male header, two wide M2 washers (approximately 8 mm outside diameter), a soldered QT pigtail and one cable strain-relief tie. It supports the sensor through its pins, keeping pressure off its optical chamber.

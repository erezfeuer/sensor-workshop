Screen & connection

D0 changes the screen. D1 opens the phone connection. D2 starts or pauses logging. Any button wakes the device; D1 also opens Wi‑Fi. Hold D0 to sleep.

CO₂ sensor settings

The sensor keeps measuring between log records. A slower cadence saves power but changes its response and self-heating.

For the temperature offset, allow ten minutes to settle in the assembled case, then subtract a trusted reference temperature from the sensor reading.

ASC is off by default. Leave it off for a greenhouse or any experiment without regular fresh-air exposure. Pausing into deep sleep removes sensor power.

Estimated average battery current —

Assumes battery operation, without USB charging. The range uses 0.7×–1.5× estimated current; it is a sensitivity range, not a measured confidence interval. Battery ageing, temperature, regulator dropout, Wi‑Fi traffic and board LEDs can change the result.

Source figures and model

Sensirion specifies 19 mA at a 2-second cadence and gives 5.6 mA at 30 seconds in its low-power example. Between 2–60 seconds this calculator uses the approximation 4.643 + 28.714 / cadence mA. Changing the sensor cadence requires recalibration.

The default sleeping-board allowance is 0.1 mA. The RTC contributes 0.2 mA while the main rail is on; its coin cell takes over when that rail is off. Light-sensor sleep and display sleep allowances add 0.024 mA. Breakout and active-board figures are provisional budgets, not measurements of this assembled device.

Waiting for storage

Storage estimate uses actual free space, 64 bytes per record and a 32 KiB reserve. Settings, clock and calibration events also use records. The logger stops adding records when full; it does not overwrite old observations. Deselecting measurements leaves blank columns and does not shrink records.

Calibrate with a reference

A guided process. Nothing is applied automatically.

01 · Prepare

Use air with a known CO₂ value

Place the device beside a trusted reference, in stable, well-mixed air. Keep your breath away from both sensors.

Do not assume that any room—or any outdoor location—is exactly 400 ppm. Forced calibration changes the sensor’s stored correction.

02 · Stabilise, then apply

Allow six minutes

The sensor stays at the selected cadence. Wi‑Fi remains available during stabilisation. Finish within ten minutes after the countdown; closing Wi‑Fi cancels the procedure. A sensor interruption restarts the countdown; the session ends after 20 minutes at most.
